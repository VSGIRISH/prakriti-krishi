From Farm to Your Doorstep 🚜📦🏡

Every Prakriti Krishi Farms product travels a carefully managed journey — freshly harvested, quality-checked, and delivered with care so you get nothing but the best.

Our Farm-to-Home Journey

🌾

Step 1 — Harvested at the Farm

Our partner farmers across India handpick or harvest produce using organic, chemical-free practices. Every crop is grown in nutrient-rich soil with care for the land and the community.

🔬

Step 2 — Quality Checked

Products arrive at our regional processing centres where they undergo rigorous quality and freshness testing. NABL-accredited labs verify purity, pesticide-free status, and shelf life.

🏪

Step 3 — Packed at Our Store

Approved products are packed in eco-friendly, food-safe packaging at our fulfilment centres. Perishable items go into insulated cold-chain packaging to maintain freshness during transit.

🚚

Step 4 — Shipped to You

Orders are dispatched via trusted logistics partners. You'll receive a tracking link by SMS/email as soon as your package leaves our centre.

🏡

Step 5 — Delivered at Your Door

Your order arrives fresh and safe. Subscription deliveries follow your chosen schedule — daily, alternate days, weekly, or custom days.

Good to Know

📦 Track Your Order

Once dispatched, you'll receive an SMS and email with a live tracking link. You can also check order status from your Account → Orders page.

🧊 Cold-Chain Packaging

Perishable items (milk, paneer, curd, eggs, seasonal fruits) are shipped in insulated, temperature-controlled packaging to ensure freshness right to your door.

Not satisfied? Unopened non-perishable products can be returned within 7 days. Damaged perishables will be replaced — just reach out via Contact Us.
