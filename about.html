Our Story

From natural farmers' fields to your family — how Prakriti Krishi Farms is redefining honest, organic commerce.

👨‍🌾🌾

From Farm to Your Home

Prakriti Krishi Farms was founded on a simple belief — that everyone deserves access to pure, chemical-free products grown the way nature intended. We work directly with over 200 small-scale organic farmers across Maharashtra, Karnataka, Kerala, and Rajasthan.

Every product on our store begins its journey on a real farm, tended by hands that understand the soil. We eliminate middlemen, ensuring that farmers earn fair prices and you receive the freshest produce at honest rates.

Our cold-chain logistics and careful packaging ensure that the goodness from farm reaches your table without compromise — whether it's raw honey from Sundarbans, cold-pressed coconut oil from Kerala, or farm-fresh A2 milk delivered to your door every morning.

🧪✅

Quality You Can Trust

Every batch is lab-tested for purity, heavy metals, and pesticide residue before it reaches our warehouse. We follow strict FSSAI guidelines and our organic products carry certifications from India Organic, USDA Organic, and Jaivik Bharat.

Our Ayurvedic products are formulated in collaboration with practitioners who combine centuries-old wisdom with modern safety standards. No parabens, no synthetic fragrances, no shortcuts.

🌍♻️

Sustainability at Our Core

We use compostable packaging made from sugarcane bagasse and recycled kraft paper. Our delivery fleet in metro cities is transitioning to electric vehicles, and we offset our carbon footprint through tree-planting drives in partnership with Grow-Trees.com.

When you shop at Prakriti Krishi Farms, you're not just buying a product — you're supporting regenerative agriculture, rural livelihoods, and a cleaner planet.
