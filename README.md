# Prakriti Krishi

Farm-fresh & natural wellness e-commerce website built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Features

- **Product Catalog**: Herbs, skincare, haircare, wellness products + bundle packs
- **Smart Search**: Autocomplete search across products, bundles, and categories
- **Advanced Filters**: Price range, stock availability, and smart tag filters
- **Smart Tags**: Best Seller, New, Most Bought, Limited Stock badges
- **Bundle Packs**: Curated bundles (veggie box, fruit box, immunity kit, etc.)
- **Subscriptions**: Recurring delivery setup for daily essentials & seasonal fruits
- **Recipes & DIY**: Recipes using store products with "Add All to Cart"
- **Harvest Calendar**: Seasonal availability of crops by month
- **User Accounts**: Register, login, address management, order history
- **Delivery Tracking**: 5-stage order tracking timeline
- **Delivery Preferences**: Call before delivery, leave at door, preferred time
- **Smart Upsell**: "You May Also Need" and "People Also Bought" recommendations
- **One-Tap Reorder**: Re-add past order items to cart instantly
- **Behavior Tracking**: Personalized "Today's Essentials" based on purchase history
- **Referral System**: Referral codes + wallet credits (₹100 each)
- **Reviews & FAQ**: Customer reviews with star ratings + collapsible FAQ
- **Mobile Responsive**: Tablet (768px) and phone (480px) breakpoints

## Pages

- `index.html` — Homepage
- `category.html` — Shop with filters
- `product.html` — Product detail page
- `cart.html` — Cart with upsell recommendations
- `checkout.html` — Checkout with delivery preferences
- `account.html` — Account dashboard (7 tabs)
- `subscriptions.html` — Recurring deliveries
- `recipes.html` — Recipes & DIY
- `harvest.html` — Harvest calendar
- `about.html` — Our story
- `reviews.html` — FAQ & Reviews
- `shipping.html` — Shipping info
- `contact.html` — Contact us

## Tech Stack

Pure HTML5 + CSS3 + Vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, no build tools.  
Data stored in `localStorage` / `sessionStorage`.

## Currency

INR (₹) with Indian locale formatting.
